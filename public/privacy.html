Privacy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

'92 Subaru is a Dallas Fort Worth cover band. This site exists so you can learn about the band and send us a booking request. Here is exactly what happens with your information when you do.

WHAT WE COLLECT

The booking form is the only place this site collects personal information. When you submit it, we receive and keep everything you entered — all nine fields:

First Name · Last Name · Email · Phone · Event Date · Event Type · Location/Venue · Budget · Message

Your request is delivered to the band as an email and kept in our inbox so we can follow up about your event. There is no database behind this site — the email is the only copy we hold. If you want a request deleted, reply to our follow-up email or send a new message through the form and we'll remove it.

WHO ELSE PROCESSES IT

Three services help run this site. Each receives only what's listed:

Resend — the email delivery service. It receives the nine booking-form fields above in order to deliver your request to our inbox.

Google reCAPTCHA — spam protection on the booking form. Google receives your IP address and browser signals to tell humans from bots, and may set Google cookies on this page. Its use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Umami — privacy-friendly analytics. It counts anonymized page views (no cookies, no personal identifiers) so we can see which pages get visited.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We don't sell your information, run ad trackers, or use it for anything beyond answering your booking request.
